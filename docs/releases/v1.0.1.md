# Ringworld Parallax 1.0.1

## Less scatter-rendering overhead

- Skip terrain patches that cannot contribute visible objects and avoid empty scatter-family GPU work.
- Reduce allocations and repeated patch/contact searches while preserving biome placement, three object LODs, fades and nearby tree contacts.
- Keep photo-mode scatter preparation and restoration of your gameplay settings.

## Updating and requirements

Close KSP, merge GameData into the KSP root and replace matching files. Your existing ring, biome and scatter settings are preserved. Enabled Parallax still suppresses native vegetation by default; buildings remain and the native-fallback option is retained.

Requires NivenRingworld >=1.1.8 (1.1.10 recommended), Parallax Continued and its complete upstream dependency/asset stack. The integration was tested with Continued 1.0.4 and stock scatter assets 1.0.0; other layouts are not certified. **Upstream Parallax plugins and assets are not bundled and must be installed separately.**

Set KSP's main-menu **Terrain Detail to High**, as Parallax requires. Ringworld's own quality preset can remain lower. A Kopernicus terrain-detail-change warning can indicate that its remembered setting disagrees with KSP's.

Four camera directions retained the same object LOD counts with culling enabled. Photo preparation/capture and settings restoration passed at Slow on KSP 1.12.5 Windows/D3D11. This is a scatter bridge, not Parallax terrain displacement; desktop high-preset performance is not certified.
